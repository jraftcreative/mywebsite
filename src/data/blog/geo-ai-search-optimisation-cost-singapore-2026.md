## The Short Answer

GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation, the work of getting your business cited in AI answers like Google's AI Overview, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini — is newer than SEO, so pricing is less standardised. But here's the honest anchor: **GEO cost tracks SEO cost, because most of the work *is* SEO work plus an AI-citation layer.**

In practice that means one of two shapes for a Singapore SME in 2026:
- **As a layer inside an SEO retainer** — a GEO-inclusive engagement sits in a similar band to SEO (commonly four figures a month, scaling with scope).
- **As a one-time foundation** — an audit, structured-data and llms.txt setup, and a first batch of answer-engine content, priced as a smaller fixed project.

What you won't see is an "ad spend" line. GEO is entirely organic — there's no platform to pay. The whole cost is the work. Anyone quoting you a fixed GEO price without looking at your site is guessing.

## Wait — Isn't GEO Just SEO?

Largely, yes — and that's good news for your budget. Google's own guidance is that optimising for its AI features "is still SEO." The foundations are the same ones strong SEO already needs: clean technical setup, structured data, clear question-and-answer content, and credible authority. If those are in place, a big part of GEO is already done.

That's why GEO is usually **a layer, not a second full bill**. We unpacked the relationship in [SEO vs GEO: what Singapore businesses should actually invest in](/blog/seo-vs-geo-singapore-2026/), and the fundamentals of the service itself in [what is GEO](/blog/what-is-geo-ai-search-optimisation/). The extra cost, where there is one, pays for the parts AI visibility needs that classic SEO doesn't.

## What You're Actually Paying For

The GEO-specific work — the bit beyond standard SEO — breaks down like this:

- **AI-search audit + query bank.** Mapping the real questions your buyers ask AI assistants, then testing across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overview to see where you're mentioned, where you're cited, and who gets cited instead. This is the scoreboard, and it's ongoing.
- **Machine-readable technical.** Structured data (schema), an `llms.txt` file, and making sure AI crawlers aren't blocked. Mostly one-time, with upkeep.
- **Answer-engine content.** Content built to be *extracted* — leads with the direct answer, specific verifiable facts, clear structure. More deliberate to write than a standard blog post.
- **Entity and authority building.** The biggest lever, and the slow, expensive one: consistent presence across the third-party sources AI trusts, so machines see your brand referenced the same way everywhere. This is where most of a serious GEO budget actually goes.
- **Cross-engine monitoring + reporting.** Re-running the query bank on a cadence to track whether your citation rate is climbing. AI answers aren't in your normal rank tracker, so this is separate work.

Notice the pattern: the cheap parts are technical and one-time; the ongoing cost is content and authority — exactly like SEO.

## Add-On or Standalone?

Most Singapore SMEs are best served by GEO **bundled into an SEO retainer** — you're paying once for overlapping foundations and getting both classic rankings and AI citations. A standalone GEO project makes sense in two cases: you already have strong SEO and just want the AI layer added, or you want a one-time foundational setup before committing to anything ongoing.

Either way, the money question isn't really "what's the GEO rate." It's "what scope does my business need" — and that's a consult, not a price tag.

## Why There's No Media Budget

With [paid ads](/blog/google-meta-ads-cost-singapore-2026/), a big chunk of your spend goes to Google or Meta to show the ads — stop paying, and the visibility stops. GEO has none of that. There's nothing to buy from the AI engines; you earn citations by being the clearest, most credible, most machine-readable source on your topic.

So the entire cost is the work, with no platform cut on top — and the visibility compounds rather than switching off. That's the same long-game logic as [how much SEO costs](/blog/seo-cost-singapore-2026/): slower to build than ads, but it keeps working.

## What a First GEO Engagement Usually Includes

To make the cost concrete, here's the typical shape of a first GEO engagement for a Singapore SME — the deliverables you're actually paying for:

- **A baseline AI-visibility audit** — your query bank run across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overview, recording where you're mentioned, where you're cited, and who wins instead. This becomes your scoreboard.
- **Technical foundations** — structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQ, Article), an `llms.txt` file, and a check that AI crawlers can actually read your site.
- **A first batch of answer-engine content** — a handful of pages rewritten or created to lead with the answer, carry specific verifiable facts, and be easy for a machine to extract and quote.
- **Entity groundwork** — making sure your name, description and details are consistent across the third-party sources AI leans on, so the machines see one coherent brand.
- **A monthly re-test and report** — the query bank re-run so you can see the citation rate move, not just guess.

The one-time pieces (audit, technical, entity cleanup) front-load the first month or two; the content and authority work is what continues. That split is exactly why GEO is usually priced like SEO — a bit heavier upfront, then an ongoing retainer.

## When It's Worth Paying For

Be honest with yourself about two things. First, are your buyers actually asking AI assistants about what you do? For most considered purchases in 2026, increasingly yes. Second, is a meaningful share of your category's searches getting answered by AI *without a click*? If the AI answer names a competitor and never mentions you, ranking a blue link underneath it isn't winning the customer.

If both are true, GEO earns its keep — usually as a modest layer on your existing SEO, not a second mortgage. If you're pre-SEO entirely, start there; GEO rides on those foundations. The playbook for actually earning the citations is in [how to get cited in Google's AI Overview](/blog/how-to-get-cited-google-ai-overview-singapore/).
